# Oven Almost-Smoked Pork Ribs

## Fit

- Allergy status: DF, GF/wheat-free, egg-free, peanut-free, tree-nut-free if all seasonings, liquid smoke, mustard, and sauce labels are clear.
- Label checks: liquid smoke, mustard, spice blends, BBQ sauce, broth if used.
- Robert fit: limit/treat because ribs are fattier and sauce can add sugar/sodium.
- Wife/breastfeeding fit: compatible as part of a balanced plate; avoid crash-diet portions.
- Toddler fit: prep required; cut meat small and remove bone/cartilage.
- Sodium risk: low to medium if homemade low-salt rub and light sauce; high if bottled BBQ sauce or salty seasoning blend is used.
- Added sugar risk: low if no BBQ sauce or tiny sauce; high if heavy sweet sauce is used.

## Ingredients

For 1 slab pork spareribs.

### Smoke layer

- 1 to 1 1/2 tsp liquid smoke, hickory or mesquite if available
- 1 tbsp yellow mustard or vinegar
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or plain vinegar

### Dry rub

- 1 tbsp paprika, ideally smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt, optional
- 1 to 2 tsp brown sugar, optional

### Finish sauce

Use a thin layer only:

- 1/4 to 1/2 cup BBQ sauce, or
- tomato sauce/paste plus vinegar, mustard, and spices

## Steps

1. Pat ribs dry. Remove the membrane from the bone side if it comes off easily.
2. Mix liquid smoke, mustard or vinegar, and vinegar. Rub a thin layer over the ribs.
3. Coat heavily with dry rub.
4. Rest 30 to 60 minutes, or 4 to 12 hours in the fridge for better flavor.
5. Put ribs on a rack over a sheet pan, meat side up.
6. Bake uncovered at 250°F for 1 1/2 to 2 hours. This helps form a bark-like surface.
7. Wrap ribs tightly in foil with 2 tbsp water, vinegar, broth, or apple juice. Add no more than 1/4 tsp extra liquid smoke if desired.
8. Bake wrapped at 275°F for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, until the meat pulls back from the bones and bends easily.
9. Unwrap, brush with a thin sauce layer, and finish at 425°F for 10 to 15 minutes, or broil 2 to 5 minutes while watching closely.
10. Rest 10 to 15 minutes before cutting.

## Best schedule

| Step | Time |
|---|---:|
| Rub rest | 30 min to overnight |
| Uncovered bake | 250°F, 1 1/2 to 2 hr |
| Wrapped bake | 275°F, 1 1/2 to 2 hr |
| Sauce finish | 425°F, 10 to 15 min |
| Rest | 10 to 15 min |

## Plate suggestion

Serve with broccoli, spinach/mushrooms, vinegar-style coleslaw, and a small rice or potato portion.
